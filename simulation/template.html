    How this is computed
    • The film on the wafer is solved with the project's ozone-water model: Henry's law, ozone decay against pH and temperature, the spinning-film thickness, and ozone carried outward and diffusing across the film to the resist, which takes up every molecule that arrives.
    • The dissolver is four ideal counterflow stages (Kremser equation), so it can never dissolve more ozone than the generator makes. At the design point this gives 0.9 of equilibrium.
    • The strip is stepped in time. Where the resist is gone, the wafer stops taking up ozone, so more of it reaches the resist further out. The steady model's time to clear, set by the slowest point, is shown beside it.
    • Resist yield: 1 g of ozone per gram of resist, resist 1200 kg/m³, carbon 70 % of the resist by mass (assumed). The drain value is the dissolved ozone in the water leaving the wafer edge.
    • Drawn from the CAD model. Not to scale on screen: the film is drawn 50 times thicker, the rotation 30 times slower, and the water and gas tracers are slowed so they can be followed.
